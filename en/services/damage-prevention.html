Many outdoor emergencies start with visible warning signs: a dry branch, a leaning tree, accumulated debris or an overgrown yard. Acting early helps protect the property.

Visible risksBranches over roofs, weak trees and blocked areas.
Early actionTrimming, removal or cleanup based on the situation.
